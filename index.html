What can Lama Bot do?+

Lama combines moderation, security, tickets, economy, leveling, statistics, administration and AI features in one modular Discord bot.

Can I choose which features to use?+

Yes. Enable the modules that fit your community and shape the bot around the way your server works.

Where can I get help or share feedback?+

Join the official Lama community for support, beta testing, bug reports and feature suggestions.

Is Lama Bot affiliated with Discord?+

No. Lama Bot is an independent third-party Discord application and is not affiliated with Discord Inc.
