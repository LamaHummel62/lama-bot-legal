About Lama Bot

Lama Bot is an independent all-in-one Discord bot designed to help communities manage, protect and grow their servers.

The project includes systems for moderation, security, tickets, economy, leveling, community tools, administration, statistics, logging and optional AI-powered features.

Lama Bot is continuously developed and new features may be added over time.

Lama Bot is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by Discord Inc.
